เล่าหลอน — YouTube Publisher

A personal publishing tool maintained by Pong Ekarin for uploading and scheduling Thai ghost-story videos on the เล่าหลอน YouTube channel. This tool is intended for the channel owner's use.

What the app does

  • Uploads videos with titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails.
  • Configures supported video settings and scheduled publication.
  • Checks the authorized channel before uploading.

Google authorization

The app requests the youtube.upload permission to upload videos and set thumbnails, and youtube.readonly to read the authorized channel's ID and name for destination verification. Access requires the owner's approval through Google's authorization screen.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for information about Google data access, local storage, and revoking authorization.
