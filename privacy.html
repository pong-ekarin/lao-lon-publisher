Privacy Policy

This policy applies to เล่าหลอน — YouTube Publisher, a personal tool maintained by Pong Ekarin.

Purpose

The app is used by the channel owner to upload and schedule Thai ghost-story videos on YouTube.

Google data access

The app requests these permissions with the owner's consent:

  • youtube.upload: Upload videos, configure supported video settings, and set thumbnails.
  • youtube.readonly: Read the authorized channel's ID and name to verify the upload destination.

How data is used

Channel information is used to check that uploads go to the intended channel. Videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, and publishing settings are sent to YouTube when the owner initiates an upload. YouTube's responses are used to report upload results and verify settings.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored locally in the owner's project workspace. They are retained for subsequent uploads until the owner deletes them. These files are not published on this website. Uploaded videos and their metadata are stored by YouTube and can be managed through YouTube Studio.

Data sharing

The app communicates with Google and YouTube to authorize access and perform publishing operations. It does not sell Google user data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train artificial intelligence models. Google user data is used only for the publishing functions described here.

Revoking access and deleting local data

The owner can revoke the app's authorization through Google Account third-party connections . Locally stored authorization can be removed by deleting .local/publishing/youtube_token.json. Deleting the local token does not revoke Google's authorization or delete videos already uploaded to YouTube.

Google API Services compliance

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements.
